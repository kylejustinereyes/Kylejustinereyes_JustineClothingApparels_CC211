Natural Fiber Care Guidelines

Machine wash cold on a gentle cycle with mild liquid detergent. Reshape while damp and line dry in the shade to preserve fiber tensile strength and prevent shrinkage.

Hand wash cool with pH-neutral wool wash, press water gently in a towel without wringing, and dry flat on a mesh rack. Fold rather than hang to maintain shoulder structure.

Turn inside out before soaking in cold water. Hang dry away from direct sunlight to retain deep indigo saturation and promote custom crease fading.
